A tabletop campaign that waits for you

Your group plays by email, at whatever pace suits. Miss a week and nothing is taken from you — no stat loss, no lingering injury, no standing to fall behind on. Your character steps offscreen and rests. When you come back you get a recap, and the party takes you straight back in. The world keeps turning either way.

The one thing showing up buys is story: the people who played are the ones the chronicle is about. That is the only difference, and it is on purpose.

No password. We send a link that works once.

If the email doesn't arrive

  1. Give it a minute, then check your spam or junk folder. The mail comes from dm@cortech.online.
  2. Add that address to your contacts. It is the same address your turns will come from, so it is worth doing once.
  3. Ask for another link — the button above works as many times as you need, and each link replaces the last.
  4. Still nothing? Some corporate and school mail systems drop unknown senders outright. A personal address is the reliable fallback, and the host can re-invite you at it.

    How this works
    1. Write what your character does, in plain language — "I follow the tracks into the marsh", not a command. There is no syntax to learn.
    2. Or just reply to the email. Every turn is sent to your inbox and replying to it is exactly the same as typing here. You never have to open this page.
    3. The turn resolves when enough of the group has acted, or when the deadline passes — whichever comes first. You are never the one holding it up.
    4. If you are busy, nothing bad happens. Your character acts in character for a turn or two, then steps offscreen and rests. Come back whenever; you get a recap and the party brings you back in level with everyone else.
